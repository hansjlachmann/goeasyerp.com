Looking for pilot customers

We are looking for pilot customers who want to be a part of our journey.

  • The product is still in alpha, and we are planning the first pilot production in January 2027.
  • More pilots are welcome to apply.
  • The business must be small, ideally 1 to 5 users.
  • We are working for free (pro bono) for the first pilot customers.
  • If your language is not supported, it will be added on demand.

Send us an email for further information: contact@goeasyerp.org.
