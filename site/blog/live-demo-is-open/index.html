The GoEasyERP live demo is open

11 November 2026

The GoEasyERP live demo is open. It is a full installation filled with sample customers, vendors, items and postings, so you can create a sales order, post a journal and look through the ledger entries without installing anything.

The sign-in details are on the demo page.

The demo data is rebuilt regularly, so feel free to try anything, but please don't enter real business data. Feedback is very welcome on GitHub.
