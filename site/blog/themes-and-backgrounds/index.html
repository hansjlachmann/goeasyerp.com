Change the theme and background in GoEasyERP

11 November 2026

GoEasyERP comes with a dark and a light theme, so you can work in the look that suits you and your screen.

To change the theme, click the Appearance menu item and choose the theme there.

You can also change the background of the main menu, in the same Appearance menu. Here the menu is shown with a background image.
