Cloud ERP and vendor lock-in vs open-source ERP

19 November 2026

In this video I share my thoughts and ask a simple question: who does the cloud ERP model really benefit, the customer or the vendor?

I have worked with ERP systems for more than 25 years, and in that time most small businesses have moved their ERP to the cloud, paying for it per user, per month. It looks convenient at the start. But the vendor sets the price and raises it when it chooses, the customer’s data lives on the vendor’s servers under the vendor’s terms, and updates arrive on the vendor’s schedule, whether they help your business or not. The longer you stay, the more it costs to leave.

I also look at what open source can and cannot solve, and explain what this project is about.
