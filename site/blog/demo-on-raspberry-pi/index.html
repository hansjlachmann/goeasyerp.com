The live demo runs on a Raspberry Pi 5

11 November 2026

The live GoEasyERP demo environment runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 with 16 GB RAM and an external SSD. Here it is, next to a one-euro coin for scale.

It runs Debian GNU/Linux 13 (trixie) and Kubernetes (k3s).

Try it out on the demo page.
