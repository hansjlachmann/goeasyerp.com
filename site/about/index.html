About

GoEasyERP

GoEasyERP is an open-source ERP for small businesses, free under the MIT licence, with no licence fees and no per-user charges. It is developed by ERP Development AS, and the project earns its living from consulting hours, not licences.

The code is on GitHub, and you can try it in the live demo.
